## 1. The short version

This site collects as little as possible. There is no analytics, no tracking pixels, no ad networks and no social media embeds. No profile of you is built and no data goes to third parties for marketing. Only what you type into a form ever leaves your browser.

That is also why you see no cookie banner: no cookie requiring consent is placed. See section 5.

## 2. Contact form

**What is collected:** your name, your email address, the text of your message and the IP address you send it from.

**Why:** to answer your question and, if it turns into a project, to prepare and carry out the agreement.

**Legal basis:** performance of a contract or steps prior to it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The IP address is processed on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): without it the form cannot be protected against abuse.

**Where it goes:** the message is emailed to my own mailbox. It is not stored in a database or CRM.

**How long:** enquiries that do not lead to a project are kept in my mailbox for at most two years. If it does lead to a project, correspondence is kept for seven years, because Dutch tax law requires that retention period for business records.

**Abuse protection:** the form contains a hidden field that only automated fillers see, measures how long you took to fill it in, and allows at most five submissions per hour per IP address. Those counters live only in the server's working memory and are cleared on restart. Nothing about you is written to disk.

## 3. Reviews

**What is collected:** the name and company you enter yourself, your rating, your review text and the IP address you send it from.

**Why:** to display the review on the site.

**Legal basis:** consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You give it by submitting the review, knowing that your name and company become publicly visible. If you would rather not be identifiable, leave out your surname or company name.

**Publication:** reviews do not go online automatically. I read every submission and verify that the sender was in fact a client. Reviews that fail that check are not published. I do not pay for reviews and I do not edit the text of a published one.

**How long:** until you ask for removal. One email is enough and I take it down.

## 4. Server logs and hosting

The site runs on my own hardware in the Netherlands. There is no hosting provider with access to the data on my behalf.

The web server keeps technical logs of requested pages, including IP address, timestamp and browser type. These are needed to investigate faults and attacks (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). They are not used to track or profile visitors.

Email is delivered through an SMTP provider. That provider processes the contents of the contact form solely in order to deliver the message, under a data processing agreement.

## 5. Cookies

There is exactly one cookie on this site:

| Cookie | Purpose | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| `fw_admin` | Keeps me signed in to the admin page | 30 days |

It is only set when *I* sign in at `/admin`. As a visitor you never receive it. It is strictly necessary for a service explicitly requested by the user, and therefore exempt from the consent requirement of Article 11.7a of the Dutch Telecommunications Act (implementing the ePrivacy Directive). No cookie banner is required, and none is shown here just for appearances.

No analytics, functional or third-party marketing cookies are placed, and nothing is stored in your browser's localStorage.

**Fonts:** the site uses Geist and Instrument Serif. They are served from this server, not from Google Fonts. Your browser makes no connection to Google, so no IP address is shared with Google.

No data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, and there is no automated decision-making or profiling.
